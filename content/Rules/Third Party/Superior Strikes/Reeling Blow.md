You unleash the full force of your strength and size in a weighty strike. Make an attack roll against a creature within your reach. On a hit, the target suffers the attack’s normal effects and takes extra damage equal to your proficiency bonus. In addition, until the end of its next turn, the creature has [[disadvantage]] on all attack rolls, and on ability checks and saving throws that use Dexterity or Intelligence, and it can’t take [[reaction|reactions]]. In addition, if this attack causes a creature to make a Constitution saving throw to maintain its [[concentration]], the creature has disadvantage on the saving throw.
