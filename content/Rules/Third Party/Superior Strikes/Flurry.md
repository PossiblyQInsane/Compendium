In a blistering display of speed, you make a number of [[unarmed strike|unarmed strikes]] equal to 1 plus half your proficiency bonus (rounded down) against creatures within your reach. Each unarmed strike must be against a different target. You then gain a +2 bonus to AC until the start of your next turn.
