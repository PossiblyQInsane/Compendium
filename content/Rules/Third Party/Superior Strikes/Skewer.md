In a thrusting strike, you attempt to skewer an enemy with your weapon. Make an attack roll against a creature within your reach. On a hit, the target suffers the attack’s normal effects and is also [[grappled]] by you. This grapple ends early if you use the weapon to make another attack or you let go of the weapon. When the grapple ends, the creature takes piercing damage equalling a number of d8s equal to your proficiency bonus.
