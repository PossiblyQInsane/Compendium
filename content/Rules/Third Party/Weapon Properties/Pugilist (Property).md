For creatures that roll a die when they make [[unarmed strike|unarmed strikes]] (such as the [[monk]] or [[Tavern Brawler (Subclass)|tavern brawler fighter]]), the size of their damage die is increased by one (maximum 1d12) when they make unarmed strikes with a hand equipped with a weapon that has this property.
