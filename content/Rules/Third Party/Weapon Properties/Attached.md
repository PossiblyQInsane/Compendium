You can’t be disarmed of this weapon, but donning or doffing the weapon takes an action. You can use a hand equipped with this weapon to hold items, but can’t attack with the weapon while doing so. Additionally, you have [[disadvantage]] on attack rolls using other weapons held in a hand equipped with this weapon and on [[Sleight of Hand]] checks using the hand that is equipped with this weapon.
