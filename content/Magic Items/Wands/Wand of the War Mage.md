_Wand, Uncommon (+1), Rare (+2), or Very Rare (+3) (Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster)_

---

While holding this wand, you gain a bonus to spell attack rolls determined by the wand’s rarity. In addition, you ignore [[Cover|Half Cover]] when making a spell attack roll.
