_Wondrous Item, Uncommon (+1), Rare (+2), or Very Rare (+3)_

---

While wearing these wraps, you have a bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with your [[Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strikes]]. The bonus is determined by the wraps’ rarity, and those strikes deal your choice of Force damage or their normal damage type.
