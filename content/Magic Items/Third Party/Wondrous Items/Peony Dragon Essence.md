_Wondrous item, uncommon_

---

This fragrant essence is made from the tall peonies in a [[peony dragon|peony dragon’s]] lair and is popular among spellcasters. Each vial of peony dragon essence contains 1d4 + 2 doses. When you cast an abjuration spell, you can add the essence to the spell as a material component to improve one casting of the spell with one of the following benefits:

- For 1 minute, the spell does not require [[concentration]].
- You gain a +1 bonus to the spell attack roll or spell save DC of the spell.
- You can choose one additional target for the spell.
- The spell’s duration is doubled.
