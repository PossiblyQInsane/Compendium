_Prerequisite: Level 2+ Warlock_

As a Bonus Action, you can expend any number of your unexpended [[Hit Point Dice|Hit Dice]] to roll those dice, add your Charisma modifier to each die, and total the rolls. Your familiar or another Fiend you command gains that many [[Temporary Hit Points]].
