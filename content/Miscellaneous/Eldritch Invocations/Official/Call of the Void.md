_Prerequisite: Level 5+ Warlock, [[Pact of the Legion]] Invocation_

When you cast _[[Find Familiar]]_ using Pact of the Legion, you can choose to summon a **[[Fel Imp]]** familiar or a **[[Voidwalker]]**. In addition, you gain the following benefits.

_**Quick Attack.**_ As a [[Bonus Action]], you can command the familiar to take the [[Attack]] action.

_**Your Spell Attack.**_ The familiar uses your spell attack bonus for its attacks.

_**Shadow Shield.**_ When your Voidwalker familiar takes damage, you can take a [[Reaction]] to grant it [[Resistance]] against that damage.
