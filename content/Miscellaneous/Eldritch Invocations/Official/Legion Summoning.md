_Prerequisite: Level 9+ Warlock, [[Pact of the Legion]] Invocation_

You can cast _[[Planar Binding]]_ without expending a spell slot. When you do, you summon a **[[Felguard]]**, a **[[Felhound]]**, an **[[Incubus]]**, or a **[[Succubus]]**, and that Fiend becomes the target of the spell. Once you cast the spell in this way, you can’t do so again until you finish a [[Long Rest]].
