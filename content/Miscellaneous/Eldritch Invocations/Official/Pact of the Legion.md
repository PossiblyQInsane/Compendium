You learn the _[[Find Familiar]]_ spell and can cast it as a [[Magic]] action without expending a spell slot. When you cast the spell, you summon a **[[Fel Imp]]** familiar.

Additionally, when you take the [[Attack]] action, you can forgo one of your own attacks to allow your familiar to make one attack with its [[Reaction]].
