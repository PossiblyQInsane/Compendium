_Level 3 Conjuration (Osteomancy) ([[Warlock]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action\
**Range:** 10 feet\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

Your rib cage opens up violently, forming a maw that attempts to devour a creature within range. The creature must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 4d8 Piercing damage and is transported through magic to a point of your choice on the ground within 60 feet of you, where the maw reappears and regurgitates them. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage only.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The damage increases by 1d8, and the teleportation distance increases by 10 feet, for each spell slot level above 3.
