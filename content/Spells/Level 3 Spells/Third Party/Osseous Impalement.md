_Level 3 Conjuration (Osteomancy) ([[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action\
**Range:** 60 feet\
**Components:** V, S, M (a calcified bamboo stick)\
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

---

Massive bone spikes sprout under up to four creatures on the ground of your choice that you can see within range. They must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 3d10 Piercing damage and be impaled by the spike, have the [[Restrained]] condition and be held aloft 5 feet in the air for the duration. A creature Restrained in this way takes 1d10 Piercing damage at the start of each of its turns.

A creature can use its action to make a Strength ([[Athletics]]) check against your spell save DC, breaking the bone spike and freeing itself on a success.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The damage (both initial and ongoing) increases by 1d10 for each spell slot level above 3.
