_Level 5 Conjuration (Osteomancy) ([[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action\
**Range:** Self\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

---

You touch the ground beneath you and tree-like bones sprout from the ground in a 30-foot-radius, 20-foot-high [[Cylinder]] centered on you. Each creature of your choice in that area must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d8 Piercing damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

For the duration, the area becomes [[Difficult Terrain]], and when a creature moves into or within the area, it takes 2d8 Piercing damage for every 5 feet it travels; you are Immune to this effect. The forest of dread provides [[Cover|Half Cover]] to any creatures inside the area. The bones disintegrate when the spell ends.
