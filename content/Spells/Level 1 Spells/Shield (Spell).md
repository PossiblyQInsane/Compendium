_Level 1 Abjuration ([[Sorcerer]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** [[Reaction]], which you take when you are hit by an attack roll or targeted by the _[[Magic Missile]]_ spell\
**Range:** Self\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** 1 round

---

An imperceptible barrier of magical force protects you. Until the start of your next turn, you have a +5 bonus to AC, including against the triggering attack, and you take no damage from _[[Magic Missile]]_.
