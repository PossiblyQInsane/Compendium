_Level 4 Illusion (Osteomancy) ([[Bard]], [[Warlock]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action\
**Range:** Self\
**Components:** V, S, M (a flute made of a femur worth 50+ GP)\
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

---

You magically create the horrifying illusion of all your bones breaking and your body bending in impossible ways, accompanied by the sound of a shattered skull. Each creature of your choice in a 30-foot-radius [[Sphere]] centered on you must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the [[Frightened]] condition for the duration. While Frightened by this spell, a creature’s [[Speed]] becomes 0 and it has the [[Prone]] condition, its legs giving out under fear. If an affected creature takes damage, it can repeat the saving throw at the end of its next turn, ending the effect on itself on a success.
