_Level 1 Conjuration ([[Druid]], [[Ranger]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action\
**Range:** 120 feet\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 hour

---

You create a 20-foot-radius [[Sphere]] of fog centered on a point within range. The Sphere is [[Heavily Obscured]]. It lasts for the duration or until a strong wind (such as one created by _[[Gust of Wind]]_) disperses it.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The fog’s radius increases by 20 feet for each spell slot level above 1.
