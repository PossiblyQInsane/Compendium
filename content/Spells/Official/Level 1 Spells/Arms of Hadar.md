_Level 1 Conjuration ([[Warlock]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action\
**Range:** Self\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

Invoking Hadar, you cause tendrils to erupt from yourself. Each creature in a 10-foot [[Emanation]] originating from you makes a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, a target takes 2d6 Necrotic damage and can’t take [[Reaction|Reactions]] until the start of its next turn. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage only.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
