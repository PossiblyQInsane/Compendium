_Level 1 Divination ([[Cleric]], [[Paladin]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action\
**Range:** Self\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

---

For the duration, you sense the location of any Aberration, Celestial, Elemental, Fey, Fiend, or Undead within 30 feet of yourself. You also sense whether the _[[Hallow]]_ spell is active there and, if so, where.

The spell is blocked by 1 foot of stone, dirt, or wood; 1 inch of metal; or a thin sheet of lead.
