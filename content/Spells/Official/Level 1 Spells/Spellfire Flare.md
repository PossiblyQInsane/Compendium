_Level 1 Evocation ([[Sorcerer]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action\
**Range:** 60 feet\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

You unleash a blast of brilliant fire. Make a ranged spell attack against a target within range; a target gains no benefit from [[Cover|Half Cover]] or [[Cover|Three-Quarters Cover]] for this attack roll. On a hit, the target takes 2d10 Radiant damage.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ You create an additional blast for each spell slot level above 1. You can direct the blasts at the same target or at different ones. Make a separate attack roll for each blast.
