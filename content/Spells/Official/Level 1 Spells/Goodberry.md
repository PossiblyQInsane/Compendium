_Level 1 Conjuration ([[Druid]], [[Ranger]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action\
**Range:** Self\
**Components:** V, S, M (a sprig of mistletoe)\
**Duration:** 24 hours

---

Ten berries appear in your hand and are infused with magic for the duration. A creature can take a [[Bonus Action]] to eat one berry. Eating a berry restores 1 [[Hit Points|Hit Point]], and the berry provides enough nourishment to sustain a creature for one day.

Uneaten berries disappear when the spell ends.
