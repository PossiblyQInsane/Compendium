_Level 2 Conjuration ([[Bard]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (a deck of cards)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

You spray a 15-foot [[Cone|cone]] of spectral cards. Each creature in that area must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 2d10 force damage and has the [[Blinded|blinded]] condition until the end of its next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage only.

_**At Higher Levels.**_ When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 2nd.
