_Level 2 Transmutation ([[Artificer]], [[Bard]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

Choose an area of nonmagical flame that you can see and that fits within a 5-foot [[Cube|cube]] within range. You can extinguish the fire in that area, and you create either fireworks or smoke when you do so.

_**Fireworks.**_ The target explodes with a dazzling display of colors. Each creature within 10 feet of the target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or become [[Blinded|blinded]] until the end of your next turn.

_**Smoke.**_ Thick black smoke spreads out from the target in a 20-foot radius, moving around corners. The area of the smoke is [[Heavily Obscured|heavily obscured]]. The smoke persists for 1 minute or until a strong wind disperses it.
