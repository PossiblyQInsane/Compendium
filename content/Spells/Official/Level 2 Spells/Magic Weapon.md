_Level 2 Transmutation ([[Artificer|Artificer]], [[Paladin]], [[Ranger]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** [[Bonus Action]]
**Range:** Touch\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** 1 hour

---

You touch a nonmagical weapon. Until the spell ends, that weapon becomes a magic weapon with a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls. The spell ends early if you cast it again.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The bonus increases to +2 with a level 3–5 spell slot. The bonus increases to +3 with a level 6+ spell slot.
