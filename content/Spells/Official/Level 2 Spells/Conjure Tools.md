_Level 2 Conjuration ([[Artificer]], [[Bard]], [[Ranger]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 8 hours

---

This spell creates a set of [[Artisan's Tools]] of your choice. You have proficiency with these tools. At the end of the spell’s duration, the tools vanish, but any items created or transformed by the tools remain. The spell ends early if you cast it again or if the tools are more than 10 feet away from you.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ If cast with a level 3 spell slot, you work as efficiently as two characters when determining how much time it takes to craft nonmagical items using the tools.
