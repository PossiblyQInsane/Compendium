_Level 6 Abjuration ([[Druid]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

---

You have [[resistance]] to acid, cold, fire, lightning, and thunder damage for the spell’s duration.

When you take damage of one of those types, you can use your [[reaction]] to gain [[immunity]] to that type of damage, including against the triggering damage.

If you do so, the [[resistance|resistances]] end, and you have the [[immunity]] until the end of your next turn, at which time the spell ends.
