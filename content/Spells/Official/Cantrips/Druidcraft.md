_Transmutation Cantrip ([[Druid]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action\
**Range:** 30 feet\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

Whispering to the spirits of nature, you create one of the following effects within range.

_**Weather Sensor.**_ You create a Tiny, harmless sensory effect that predicts what the weather will be at your location for the next 24 hours. The effect might manifest as a golden orb for clear skies, a cloud for rain, falling snowflakes for snow, and so on. This effect persists for 1 round.

_**Bloom.**_ You instantly make a flower blossom, a seed pod open, or a leaf bud bloom.

_**Sensory Effect.**_ You create a harmless sensory effect, such as falling leaves, spectral dancing fairies, a gentle breeze, the sound of an animal, or the faint odor of skunk. The effect must fit in a 5-foot [[Cube]].

_**Fire Play.**_ You light or snuff out a candle, a torch, or a campfire.
