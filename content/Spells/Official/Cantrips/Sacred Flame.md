_Evocation Cantrip ([[Cleric]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action\
**Range:** 60 feet\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

Flame-like radiance descends on a creature that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d8 Radiant damage. The target gains no benefit from [[Cover|Half Cover]] or [[Cover|Three-Quarters Cover]] for this save.

_**Cantrip Upgrade.**_ The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).
