_Evocation Cantrip ([[Artificer]], [[Bard]], [[Druid]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action\
**Range:** Self\
**Components:** S\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

Each creature in a 5-foot [[Emanation]] originating from you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take 1d6 Thunder damage. The spell’s thunderous sound can be heard up to 100 feet away.

_**Cantrip Upgrade.**_ The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).
