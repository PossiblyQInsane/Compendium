_Level 7 Transmutation ([[Druid]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** [[Bonus Action]]
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (a statuette of a dragon, worth at least 500 gp)
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

---

With a roar, you draw on the magic of dragons to transform yourself, taking on draconic features. You gain the following benefits until the spell ends:

> [!NOTE|no-i no-t bg-plain]
> **Blindsight.** You have [[blindsight]] with a range of 30 feet. Within that range, you can effectively see anything that isn’t behind [[cover|total cover]], even if you’re [[blinded]] or in [[darkness]]. Moreover, you can see an [[invisible]] creature, unless the creature successfully hides from you.
>
> **Breath Weapon.** When you cast this spell, and as a [[bonus action]] on subsequent turns for the duration, you can exhale shimmering energy in a 60-foot [[cone]]. Each creature in that area must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 6d8 force damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
>
> **Wings.** Incorporeal wings sprout from your back, giving you a [[fly speed|flying speed]] of 60 feet.
