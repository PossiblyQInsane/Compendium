_Level 3 Abjuration ([[Artificer|Artificer]], [[Bard]], [[Cleric]], [[Druid]], [[Paladin]], [[Ranger]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action\
**Range:** 120 feet\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

Choose one creature, object, or magical effect within range. Any ongoing spell of level 3 or lower on the target ends. For each ongoing spell of level 4 or higher on the target, make an ability check using your spellcasting ability (DC 10 plus that spell’s level). On a successful check, the spell ends.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ You automatically end a spell on the target if the spell’s level is equal to or less than the level of the spell slot you use.
