_Level 3 Enchantment ([[Artificer]], [[Bard]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** S, M (a pinch of sand)
**Duration:** 10 minutes

---

You make a calming gesture, and up to three willing creatures of your choice that you can see within range fall [[Unconscious|unconscious]] for the spell’s duration. The spell ends on a target early if it takes damage or someone uses an action to shake or slap it awake. If a target remains unconscious for the full duration, that target gains the benefit of a [[Short Rest|short rest]], and it can’t be affected by this spell again until it finishes a [[Long Rest|long rest]].

_**At Higher Levels.**_ When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, you can target one additional willing creature for each slot level above 3rd.
