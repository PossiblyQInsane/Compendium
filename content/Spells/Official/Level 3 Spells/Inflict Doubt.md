_Level 3 Enchantment ([[Bard]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 120 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

---

You inflict self-doubt on a creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have [[Disadvantage]] on [[D20 Test|D20 Tests]] for the duration. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.
