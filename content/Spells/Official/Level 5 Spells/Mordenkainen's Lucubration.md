_Level 5 Divination ([[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

You recover up to two expended spell slots of level 2 or lower.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The maximum level of spell slots you can recover increases to level 3 (if cast with a level 6–7 spell slot) or level 4 (if cast with a level 8+ spell slot).
