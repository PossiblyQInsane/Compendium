_Level 9 Evocation ([[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

You create a 30-foot [[Cone]] of oblivion. Each [[Bloodied]] creature in the Cone makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save a creature dies, and it and everything nonmagical it is wearing and carrying are turned to dust. The target can be revived only by a _[[True Resurrection]]_ or a _[[Wish]]_ spell.
