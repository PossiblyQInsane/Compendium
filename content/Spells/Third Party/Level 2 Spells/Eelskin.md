_Level 2 Biomancy\* ([[Druid]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Tamer]], [[Warlock]], [[Wizard]]) (\*can be replaced with evocation)_

---

**Casting Time:** Action or [[Bonus Action]]
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (some jellied eel)
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

---

You cause the skin of a willing creature you touch to produce a slimy, electrified mucous. The creature’s [[unarmed strike|unarmed strikes]] and natural weapon attacks become magical for the duration of the spell. The first time the creature hits with an unarmed strike or an attack using a natural weapon on its turn, it deals an additional **1d8** lightning damage as the stored lightning discharges.

**_Slimy._** For the duration, the creature has [[advantage]] on checks and saves made to avoid being [[grappled]] or [[restrained]], and [[disadvantage]] on any attempts it makes to grapple another creature.

**_At Higher Levels._** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the damage increases by **1d8** for each two slot levels above 2nd.
