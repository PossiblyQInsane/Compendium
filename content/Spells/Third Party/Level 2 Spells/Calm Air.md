_Level 2 Transmutation ([[Bender]] (air), [[Druid]], [[Ranger]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** [[Bonus Action]]
**Range:** 120 feet (60-foot [[cube]])
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

---

With a calming gesture and soothing word, you calm the air in a 60-foot [[cube]] centred on a point within range for the duration. In the area, wind becomes still, the flow of poisonous gases is halted, and the effects of air-based spells of a level equal to or lower than this spell, such as the _[[Wind Drake]]_ spell, are prevented and suppressed.

_**At Higher Levels.**_ When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the level of air-based spells suppressed by this spell increases.
