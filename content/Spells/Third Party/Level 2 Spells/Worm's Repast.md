_Level 2 Necromancy ([[Druid]], [[Ranger]], [[Warlock]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You seed spectral, gnawing worms into the flesh of a creature you can see within range. The creature makes a Constitution saving throw. Undead creatures with flesh, such as **[[zombie|zombies]]**, make the save with [[Disadvantage]]. On a failed save, the target takes 4d6 Piercing damage, and the worms continue to gnaw on the target for the duration. While the worms remain, the target takes an additional 2d6 Piercing damage at the end of each of its turns. On a successful save, the target takes half as much damage and worms disappear immediately.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The damage (both initial and later) increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 2.
