_Level 2 Transmutation (Sangromancy) ([[Druid]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** [[Reaction]], which you take when a creature you can see within 30 feet of you takes damage
**Range:** Self\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

As part of casting this spell, you must expend two [[Hit Point Dice]] or the spell automatically fails.

Roll the Hit Point Dice expended to cast the spell. The triggering creature takes Necrotic damage equal to the result. You gain [[Temporary Hit Points]] equal to the result plus the triggering damage, to a maximum of 15 Temporary Hit Points.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The maximum number of Temporary Hit Points you can gain from casting increases by 10 for each slot level above 2.
