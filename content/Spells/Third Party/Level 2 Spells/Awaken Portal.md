_Level 2 Conjuration ([[Bard]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** 1 hour or [[Ritual]]
**Range:** 10 feet
**Components:** V, M (chalk and ink)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

You attempt to reactivate a dormant gate, portal, or _[[Teleportation Circle]]_ by infusing it with magical energies. This spell must be cast twice, once at each location the portal connects. While the _Teleportation Circles_ and portals found in the Abomination Vaults require no additional cost to awaken, other portals might require rare items or specific materials as a cost for this spell at the GM’s discretion—some portals might even be so powerful that this spell cannot awaken them at all.

If the other side of the portal is already awakened, the portal remains open for 30 days. If you cast the spell at both ends of the portal every month for one year, the portal remains open permanently. If the other end is not yet awakened, this side remains awakened for 10 days but isn’t usable unless the other end is awakened within that time. Whenever you finish a [[Long Rest]] with only one of a pair of portals awakened, there is a risk that a dangerous entity takes advantage of the interdimensional opening. You must make a Charisma saving throw against a DC determined by the GM and varying depending on the location and nature of the portal. In the Abomination Vaults, the DC is 10 plus the dungeon level where the portal is located. Thus, the portal on the first level of the Abomination Vaults has a save DC of 11, while the portal on the fourth level is DC 14.

On a failed save, you are haunted by the dangerous entity, and your spellcasting ability score is reduced by 1d4 until you finish a [[Long Rest]]. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, instead the entity breaks through and immediately seeks out whoever awakened the portal. The entity magically senses your location at the time it arrives and for the next 24 hours. The creature drawn through the portal varies according to the portal’s nature.
