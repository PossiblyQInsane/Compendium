_Level 2 Biomancy\* ([[Bender]] (earth), [[Druid]], [[Ranger]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Tamer]], [[Wizard]]) (\*can be replaced by transmutation)_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (a carved stone figurine)
**Duration:** 10 minutes

---

At your touch, the skin of a willing creature hardens with stone-like fortification. Its speed is reduced by 5 feet and, when you cast this spell and at the start of each of the target’s turns, it gains 2d4 [[temporary hit points]]. When the spell ends, the target loses any remaining temporary hit points granted by this spell.

_**At Higher Levels.**_ When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 2nd.
