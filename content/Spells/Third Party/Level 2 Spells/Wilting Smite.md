_Level 2 Transmutation (Sangromancy) ([[Paladin]])_

---

**Casting Time:** [[Bonus Action]], which you take immediately after hitting a creature with a Melee weapon or an [[Unarmed Strike]]\
**Range:** Self\
**Components:** V\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

As part of casting this spell, you must expend two [[Hit Point Dice]] or the spell automatically fails. The creature loses all [[Resistance|Resistances]] to damage until the start of your next turn. The target hit by the strike takes extra Necrotic damage from the attack. To determine this damage, roll the Hit Point Dice expended to cast the spell.
