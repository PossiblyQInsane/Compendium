_Level 2 Divination ([[Bard]], [[Cleric]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 1 mile
**Components:** S, M (a glass eye)
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

---

Choose a willing creature you’re familiar with; the spell fails if the target isn’t within range. For the duration, or until the target dismisses the spell as a [[Magic]] action, it can see through your eyes, gaining the benefits of any special senses you have. Until the spell ends, the target can swap between its own vision and yours as a [[Bonus Action]].

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ You can target one additional willing creature for each spell slot level above 2.
