_Level 2 Transmutation ([[Bender]] (air), [[Paladin]], [[Ranger]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

With a blast of air, you leap up to 30 feet before making one [[unarmed strike]] or melee attack with a weapon you are holding. You can make two attacks if you have the Extra Attack class feature. On a hit, each attack deals an extra 1d8 thunder damage.

_**At Higher Levels.**_ When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the distance you can leap increases by 15 feet, and each attack deals an extra 1d8 thunder damage on a hit, for each slot level above 2nd.
