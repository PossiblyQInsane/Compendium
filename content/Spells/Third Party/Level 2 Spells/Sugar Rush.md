_Level 2 Biomancy\* ([[Bard]], [[Cleric]], [[Paladin]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Tamer]]) (\*can be replaced with transmutation)_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (some candy)
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

---

Choose a willing creature that you can see within range. Until the spell ends, the target’s speed is increased by **15 feet**, and it gains a **+1 bonus** to AC. As a [[bonus action]] on each of its turns, it can take the [[Dash]], [[Disengage]], [[Hide]], or [[Utilize]] action.

When the spell ends, or if an affected creature does not spend movement up to its walking speed by the end of its turn, it then develops a stomach ache and is [[Two-Handed]] until the end of its next turn

**_At Higher Levels._** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 2nd.
