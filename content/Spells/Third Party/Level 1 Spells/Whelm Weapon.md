_Level 1 Transmutation ([[Bard]], [[Druid]], [[Ranger]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a drop of seawater)
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

---

Up to three weapons of your choice that you can see within range become enveloped in water. Roll a d4; for the duration, any damage dealt by the weapons is reduced by the number rolled (to a minimum of 1 damage).

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ You can target two additional weapons for each spell slot level above 1.
