_Level 1 Transmutation (Osteomancy) ([[Warlock]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action\
**Range:** Touch\
**Components:** V, S, M (a porcupine’s carapace)\
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

---

You touch a willing creature, forcing spiked bone plates to grow and pierce through its skin. It takes 1 point of Piercing damage. For the duration of the spell, the first time each turn that the target is hit with a melee attack roll, the attacking creature takes 2d4 Piercing damage.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The damage increases by 1d12 for each spell slot level above 1. The spell doesn’t require [[Concentration]] with a level 5+ spell slot.
