_Level 1 Transmutation ([[Bard]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** [[Reaction]] (which you takes when you succeed on a saving throw against a spell cast by another creature, or an attack from such a spell misses you)
**Range:** Self
**Components:** S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

You chomp down on the threads of magic left behind after the triggering spell fails to affect you, swallowing the now-latent energy and gaining one of the following effects of your choice:

- You regain Hit Points equal to 1d4 + your spellcasting ability modifier.
- Your Speed increases by 15 feet and you don’t provoke [[Opportunity Attacks]] until the end of your next turn.
- Once before the end of your next turn, you can roll a d6 and add the number rolled to a [[D20 Test]] you make.
