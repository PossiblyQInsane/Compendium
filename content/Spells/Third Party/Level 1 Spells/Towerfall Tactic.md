_Level 1 Abjuration ([[Magus]])_

---

**Casting Time:** [[Reaction]], which you take when you take damage from an attack
**Range:** Self
**Components:** S, M (a [[Shield]] worth 1+ GP)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

You gain 5 [[Temporary Hit Points]] and the creature that made the triggering attack must make a Strength saving throw. On a failure, the creature gains the [[Prone]] condition and it cannot right itself to end the condition until after the end of the current turn. These Temporary Hit Points vanish if any remain after 1 minute.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The Temporary Hit Points increase by 5 for each spell slot level above 1.
