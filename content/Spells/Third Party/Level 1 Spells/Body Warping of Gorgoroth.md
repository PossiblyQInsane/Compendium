_Level 1 Transmutation ([[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** S
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

---

Choose a Humanoid of your size that you can see. You physically transform to become an exact duplicate of that creature for the duration. Even a thorough physical examination fails to find any differences between you and the target.

Your voice and equipment don’t change.
