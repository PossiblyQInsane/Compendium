_Level 1 Necromancy ([[Cleric]], [[Warlock]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a vial of fetid swamp mud)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

Negative energy crackles around your hand then shoots in a jagged black bolt from your outstretched fingers to a target of your choice that you can see within range. An Undead creature targeted by this spell regains 1d8 Hit Points, otherwise the target takes 1d8 Necrotic damage. A Humanoid creature who dies as a result of taking damage from this spell immediately transforms into a **[[Zombie]]** and acts at the start of the next round.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The Hit Points regained or damage dealt increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 1.
