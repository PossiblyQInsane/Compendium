**Range:** Touch\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

As part of casting this spell, you must expend one [[Hit Point Dice|Hit Point Die]] or the spell automatically fails. A willing target takes Fire damage equal to a roll of the expended Hit Point Die. If the target has the [[Poisoned]] condition, that condition immediately ends.
