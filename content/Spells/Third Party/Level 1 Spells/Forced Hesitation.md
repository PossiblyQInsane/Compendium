_Level 1 Enchantment ([[Bard]], [[Ranger]])_

---

**Casting Time:** [[Reaction]], which you take when a creature you can see within range makes an Initiative roll
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

You tug on the threads of a creature’s awareness at a pivotal moment. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or take 3d4 Psychic damage, and its Initiative roll is reduced by the highest result of the damage dice.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The damage increases by 1d4 for each spell slot level above 1.
