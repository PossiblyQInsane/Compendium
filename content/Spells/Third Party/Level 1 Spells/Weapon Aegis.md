_Level 1 Abjuration ([[Magus]], [[Paladin]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** S, M (an iron shaving)
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

---

You create a magical aegis with 3d8 Hit Points in an unoccupied space within range. When a creature within 10 feet of the aegis takes Bludgeoning, Piercing, Poison, or Slashing damage, you can choose for the aegis to take the damage instead. If this damage reduces the aegis to 0 Hit Points, the aegis is destroyed and the spell ends early. While this spell persists, you can use a [[Bonus Action]] to move the aegis to another unoccupied space within 60 feet of you.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The aegis’s hit points increase by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 1.
