_Level 5 Necromancy ([[Bard]], [[Warlock]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 10 minutes

---

You call upon multiple tendrils of ephemeral ectoplasm to pass through one creature you can see within range and carry away all harmful psychic afflictions. You end each spell or effect giving the target the [[Charmed]] or [[Frightened]] condition. In addition, if the target is possessed, the possession ends. Each expelled effect manifests momentarily as a colored aura or other sensory cue that imparts to you its school of magic (if any) and then vanishes in the general direction of its caster or source. For the duration, the target has [[Advantage]] on saving throws against all the expelled spells and effects (whether from the same source or a different one).
