_Level 5 Transmutation ([[Bard]], [[Paladin]], [[Ranger]], [[Warlock]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action\
**Range:** Touch\
**Components:** V, S, M (a four-leaf clover and a shard of broken mirror)\
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 hour

---

You touch a Tiny, nonmagical object, infusing it with a portion of your own luck for the duration. While another creature carries the object, that creature has [[Advantage]] on [[D20 Test|D20 Tests]], and you have [[Disadvantage]] on D20 Tests.
