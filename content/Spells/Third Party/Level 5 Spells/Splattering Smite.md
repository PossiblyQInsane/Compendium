_Level 5 Abjuration (Sangromancy) ([[Paladin]])_

---

**Casting Time:** [[Reaction]] (which you take when you hit a creature with a Melee weapon)\
**Range:** Self\
**Components:** V\
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

---

Your strikes drink the splattered blood of your foes. As part of casting this spell, you must expend five [[Hit Point Dice|Hit Dice]] or the spell automatically fails. Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with an attack roll using a Melee weapon or [[Unarmed Strike]], roll the Hit Point Dice expended to cast the spell and regain a number of Hit Points equal to the number rolled.
