_Level 5 Evocation ([[Bender]] (air), [[Druid]], [[Warlock]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (a smoke-filled crystal)
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

---

A cyclone of swirling wind encases you. For the duration, you have a [[Fly Speed|flying speed]] of 30 feet and can hover, and weapon attack rolls against you have [[disadvantage]].

As a [[bonus action]] on your turns until the spell ends, you can cause the cyclone to emit a blast of wind. Make a melee or ranged spell attack against a creature within 30 feet of you. On a hit, the creature takes 3d8 bludgeoning damage and is pushed 15 feet directly away from you.

_**At Higher Levels.**_ When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the damage of the blast of wind increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 5th.
