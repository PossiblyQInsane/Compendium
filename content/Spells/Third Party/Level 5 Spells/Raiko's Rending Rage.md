_Level 5 Transmutation ([[Bender]] (air, fire), [[Ranger]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (a piece of fulgurite)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

You imbue yourself with the swift rage of a tempest, turning your movement into a storm of thunder and lightning. Until the end of the turn, you gain 40 feet of additional movement, your movement doesn’t provoke [[opportunity attacks]], and you can make up to four melee weapon attacks (no action required), though each must be against a different target. On a hit, these attacks deal an extra 2d8 lightning damage.

At the end of the turn, a thunderous boom follows in your wake that can be heard 2,500 feet away. Each creature you attacked with this spell must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes 2d8 thunder damage and is knocked [[prone]]. On a success, a creature takes half as much damage and is not knocked prone.

_**At Higher Levels.**_ When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the lightning and thunder damage each increase by 1d8 for each slot level above 5th.
