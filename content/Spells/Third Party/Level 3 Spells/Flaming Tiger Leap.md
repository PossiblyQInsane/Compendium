_Level 3 Transmutation ([[Bender]] (fire), [[Druid]], [[Ranger]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self (15-foot-radius, 5-foot-high [[cylinder]])
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

You erupt from the ground like a firework of the [[College of Hanabi]], but thankfully without exploding at your apex. Each creature within a 15-foot-radius, 5-foot-high cylinder centred on you must make a Dexterity saving throw. A creature takes 5d6 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. Until the end of your turn, you gain a [[Fly Speed|flying speed]] of 120 feet, and your movement doesn’t provoke [[opportunity attacks]].

_**At Higher Levels.**_ When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6, and your flying speed increases by 30 feet, for each slot level above 3rd.
