_Level 3 Enchantment ([[Bard]], [[Magus]], [[Paladin]])_

---

**Casting Time:** [[Bonus Action]]
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

You imbue your voice with supernatural authority and order a creature you can see within range that can hear and understand you to attack. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or immediately use its [[Reaction]] to make an attack against a creature of your choice other than itself within the reach or normal range of an attack it can make. The target makes this save with [[Advantage]] if it is Immune to the [[Charmed]] condition. The spell fails if the target is unable to take a Reaction or if there is no viable creature for it to attack.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ If you use a spell slot of level 6+, the duration increases to “[[Concentration]], up to 1 minute”, and you can use a Bonus Action on each of your subsequent turns for the duration to rebear the spells’ effect. When you do, you can target the same creature or a different one within range.
