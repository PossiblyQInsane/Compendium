_Level 3 Conjuration ([[Paladin]], [[Ranger]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action\
**Range:** 30 feet\
**Components:** V, S, M (a melee weapon worth 1+ SP)\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

You send forth three spectral duplicates of yourself to strike down enemies within 30 feet of you. You can order them to strike one target or several. Make a melee spell attack for each spectral duplicate. On a hit, a target takes 4d8 damage of your weapon’s damage type.

You can then take a [[Bonus Action]] to move up to 30 feet in a straight line towards one of the targets without provoking [[Opportunity Attacks]], streaking through a spectral trail, and make a single melee weapon attack.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The damage of your spectral duplicate increases by 2d8 for each spell slot level above 3.
