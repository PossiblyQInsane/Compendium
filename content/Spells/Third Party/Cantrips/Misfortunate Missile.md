_Divination Cantrip ([[Bard]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a tuft of fur from a black cat)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

Misfortune crystallizes into a physical force on your finger tips and launches where you direct it. Make a ranged spell attack against a creature you can see within range. On a hit, you deal 1d6 Force damage and before the start of your next turn, immediately after the target makes a [[D20 Test]], you can use your [[Reaction]] to roll 1d4 and subtract it from the triggering roll’s result.

_**Cantrip Upgrade.**_ The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).
