_Evocation Cantrip ([[Druid]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

With a _thdoom_ that can be heard 100 feet away, you cause a sudden increase in the air pressure surrounding a creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on a **Constitution saving throw** or take **1d8** thunder damage and become [[deafened]] until the start of your next turn.

This spell’s damage increases by 1d8 when you reach 5th level (**2d8**), 11th level (**3d8**), and 17th level (**4d8**).
