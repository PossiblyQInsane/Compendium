_Necromancy Cantrip ([[Cleric]], [[Druid]], [[Magus]], [[Warlock]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

The chill of mortality rattles through a creature of your choice within range. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take 1d8 Cold damage and lose all damage [[Resistance|Resistances]] it has until the start of your next turn, or until it takes damage of a type it normally has Resistance to.

_**Cantrip Upgrade.**_ The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).
