_Transmutation Cantrip ([[Druid]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

By placing your hand on another creature, you transfer your life force to them. You expend and roll one of your [[Hit Point Dice|Hit Dice]]. The target regains a number of Hit Points equal to the result of the roll.

_**Cantrip Upgrade.**_ You can expend and roll an additional Hit Die when you reach levels 5 (2 total), 11 (3 total), and 17 (4 total).
