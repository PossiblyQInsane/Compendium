_Divination Cantrip ([[Bard]], [[Cleric]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 120 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a twenty sided die)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

You cause a creature of your choice that you can see within range to shimmer with magical energy. When you do, the target must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or take 1d6 Radiant damage and become overexposed until it takes a [[Critical Hit]] or until the start of your next turn. While overexposed, attack rolls against the target can score a Critical Hit on a roll of 19 or 20.

_**Cantrip Upgrade.**_ The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).
