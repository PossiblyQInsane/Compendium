_Enchantment Cantrip ([[Bard]], [[Magus]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (an obtusely written book)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

You cause a creature to temporarily forget everything they’ve ever learned. The target must make an Intelligence saving throw. On a failure, it takes 1d4 Psychic damage and the first time it makes a [[D20 Test]] before the end of your next turn it takes a penalty equal to its Proficiency Bonus.

_**Cantrip Upgrade.**_ The damage increases by 1d4 when you reach levels 5 (2d4), 11 (3d4), and 17 (4d4).
