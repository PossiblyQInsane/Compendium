_Conjuration Cantrip ([[Magus]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 90 feet
**Components:** S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

You thrust your hand into empty space in front of you and punch through to a demiplane housing the contents of a random _[[Bag of Holding]]_ somewhere in the multiverse, then launch whatever you can grab at a creature you can see within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target, dealing 1d8 Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage (your choice) on a hit. After you make the attack, the object teleports back to the demiplane it came from.

_**Cantrip Upgrade.**_ The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).
