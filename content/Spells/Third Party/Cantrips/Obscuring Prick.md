_Illusion Cantrip ([[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a silver needle)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

A creature of your choice within range experiences a brief flash of pain in one of its eyes. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it takes 1d4 Piercing damage and the target cannot see you, as if it had the [[Blinded]] condition, until the start of your next turn.

_**Cantrip Upgrade.**_ The damage increases by 1d4 when you reach levels 5 (2d4), 11 (3d4), and 17 (4d4).
