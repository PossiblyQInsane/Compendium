_Transmutation Cantrip ([[Druid]], [[Magus]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

You attempt to strike a creature within range, causing its vital components to turn caustic and self-destructive. Make a melee spell attack against the target. On a hit, you deal 1d4 Acid damage and the target must expend and roll a [[Hit Point Dice|Hit Dice]] if it has any. The target takes additional Poison damage equal to the result.

_**Cantrip Upgrade.**_ The Acid damage increases by 1d4 when you reach levels 5 (2d4), 11 (3d4), and 17 (4d4).
