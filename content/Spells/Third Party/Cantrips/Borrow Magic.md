_Transmutation Cantrip ([[Bard]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** [[Bonus Action]]
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** S
**Duration:** 1 round

---

You touch a willing creature, pulling from its pool of magical energy to fuel your spellcasting. The target expends a level 1 spell slot, temporarily lending it to you. Once before the start of your next turn, you can cast a spell using the borrowed spell slot instead of your own. If you don’t expend the borrowed spell slot before then, it is lost. Once a creature has lent three spell slots in this way, it can’t do so again until it finishes a [[Long Rest]].

_**Cantrip Upgrade.**_ The maximum level of spell slot that can be lent to you in this way increases when you reach levels 5 (level 2 slot), 11 (level 3 slot), and 17 (level 4 slot).
