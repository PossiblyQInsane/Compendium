_Divination Cantrip ([[Artificer]], [[Bard]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (a set of [[Artisan's Tools]] or a [[Herbalism Kit]])
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

---

You channel your arcane talent through a set of tools. Choose an Artisan’s Tool or Herbalism Kit. Until the spell ends, you add 1d6 to ability checks you make when you take the [[Utilize]] action with the chosen tool.
