_Level 4 Divination ([[Magus]], [[Ranger]])_

---

**Casting Time:** [[Bonus Action]]
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** S
**Duration:** 1 minute

---

You divine the strengths and weaknesses of a creature you can see within range. You immediately learn if the target is [[Immunity|Immune]], [[Resistance|Resistant]], or [[Vulnerability|Vulnerable]] to any damage types, and if so what types. In addition, for the duration you gain the following benefits:

- You know if the target is [[Bloodied]].
- Your attack rolls against the target score a [[Critical Hit]] on a roll of 19 or 20 on the d20.
- When the target attacks you, you can use your [[Reaction]] to give the attack [[Disadvantage]].
