_Level 4 Necromancy ([[Warlock]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 120 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a gravestone ground into powder)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

A black speck of dust floats to a point you choose within range before erupting with necrotic energy. Each Undead in a 20-foot-radius [[Sphere]] centred on that point regains 3d8 Hit Points. Other creatures and objects within the Sphere must make a Constitution saving throw, taking 6d8 Necrotic damage on a failed save. A Humanoid killed by this spell transforms into a **[[Zombie]]** which immediately acts at the start of the next round, and is hostile to all creatures, including you.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The damage and healing both increase by 1d8 for every spell slot level above 4.
