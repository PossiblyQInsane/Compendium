_Level 4 Abjuration ([[Bard]], [[Cleric]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (a spritz of perfume)
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

---

You touch a creature and refine their natural and honed talents into a perfectly calibrated machine. When you cast this spell on a willing creature, that creature rolls all ability checks in which they are proficient with [[advantage]], and treats any ability checks in which they have [[expertise]] in as a 20.

_**At Higher Levels.**_ When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, you can increase the duration by 10 minutes for each slot level above 4th.
