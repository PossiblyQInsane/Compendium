_Evocation Cantrip ([[Artificer]], [[Sorcerer]], [[Wizard]])_

---

**Casting Time:** Action\
**Range:** 120 feet\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

---

You hurl a mote of fire at a creature or an object within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 Fire damage. A flammable object hit by this spell starts [[Burning|burning]] if it isn’t being worn or carried.

_**Cantrip Upgrade.**_ The damage increases by 1d10 when you reach levels 5 (2d10), 11 (3d10), and 17 (4d10).
