**Cost:** 1 CP
**Weight:** 1 lb.

---

A Torch burns for 1 hour, casting [[Bright Light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[Dim Light]] for an additional 20 feet. When you take the [[Attack|Attack]] action, you can attack with the Torch, using it as a Simple Melee weapon. On a hit, the target takes 1 Fire damage.
