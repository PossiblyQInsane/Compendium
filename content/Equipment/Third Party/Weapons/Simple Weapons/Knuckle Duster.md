A knuckle duster is a simple melee weapon, usually made of metal or another hard material, worn around the knuckles of one hand. They deal 1d4 bludgeoning damage on a hit and have the [[Attached]], [[Light (Property)|Light]], and [[Pugilist (Property)|Pugilist]] properties.

_**Variant: Spiked Knuckle Dusters.**_ This variant on the knuckleduster has the [[Adaptable]] (piercing) property, and costs 2 gp.
