_Martial Melee Weapon_

---

**Damage:** 1d6 Bludgeoning
**Weight:** 4 lb.
**Cost:** 7 SP

---

**Properties:**

- [[Reach]]: A Reach weapon adds 5 feet to your reach when you attack with it, as well as when determining your reach for [[Opportunity Attacks]] with it.
- Special: As a [[Bonus Action]], you can choose a creature your size or smaller (minimum Medium) that you can see within your Shepherd Crook's reach. The target must make a Strength saving throw (DC 8 plus your Proficiency Bonus and Strength modifier). On a failed save, you move the target to another unoccupied space within 5 feet of you.

**[[Mastery Properties|Mastery]]:**

- [[Push]]: If you hit a creature with this weapon, you can push the creature up to 10 feet straight away from yourself if it is Large or smaller.
