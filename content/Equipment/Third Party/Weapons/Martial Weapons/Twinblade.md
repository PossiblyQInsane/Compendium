_Martial Melee Weapon_

---

**Damage:** 2d4 Slashing
**Properties:** [[Finesse]], Special: Whirl, [[Two-Handed]]
**Weight:** 5 lb.
**Cost:** 50 GP

---

> [!caption|right wsmall]
> ![[Images/Twinblade.png]]

Twinblades are martial melee weapons; double-bladed polearms that rely on the user’s dexterity to keep them in constant motion. They have the [[finesse]] and [[two-handed]] properties, and deal **2d4** slashing damage on a hit. They also have the Special: Whirl property.

**_Special: Whirl._** When you are wielding the twinblade in two hands, you can use a bonus action to attempt to whirl the weapon. To whirl, make a **DC 10 Dexterity** check, adding your proficiency bonus if you are proficient with twinblades. On a success, you gain a **+1 bonus** to your AC until the start of your next turn, the whirling blades acting as a barrier against attacks. On a result of 15 or higher, you gain a **+2 bonus**, instead. On a failure, you deal slashing damage to yourself equal to your proficiency bonus and gain no bonus to your AC from the whirl this turn.
