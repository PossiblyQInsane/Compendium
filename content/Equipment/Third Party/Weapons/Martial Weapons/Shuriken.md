Shuriken, also known as throwing stars, are martial ranged weapons—several sharp spikes or blades set in a circular pattern, usually around a central ring. They deal 1d4 piercing damage on a hit, count as monk weapons, and have the [[Finesse]], [[Light (Property)|Light]], and [[Thrown]] (range 20/60) properties.
