_Martial Melee Weapon_

---

**Damage:** 1d4 Piercing

**Weight:** 1 lb.

---

Starknives are martial melee weapons; three to five double-edged blades set on a central handle that are most effective as thrown weapons but can also be used for close combat. They deal 1d4 piercing damage on a hit, can count as a monk weapon, and have the [[Adaptable]] (slashing), [[Finesse]], [[Light (Property)|Light]], [[Thrown]] (range 20/60), and [[Thrown Versatility]] (1d6) properties.
