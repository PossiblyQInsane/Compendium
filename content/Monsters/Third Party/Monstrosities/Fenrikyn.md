_Howling Fangs and Chains_

> [!recite|bg-yellow color-white no-i] **Habitat:** Arctic, Forest, Hill; **Treasure:** Any

![[Images/Fenrikyn.png|443]]

_This massive, warped canine has frothing jaws and chains that wrap around its body, obeying its every whim._

Fenrikyn are warped worgs and winter wolves, altered by the influence of Fenris, the Great Wolf. Fenrikyn roam the cold forests and the hills of the Northlands, devouring all that they encounter. Generally solitary, fenrikyn have been known to gather into small packs or to take over a pack of wolves or worgs. Though they despise most living things, they reserve their greatest enmity for good-aligned canines and followers of the god Tyr.
