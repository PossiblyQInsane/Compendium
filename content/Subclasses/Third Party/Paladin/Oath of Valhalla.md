_Demonstrate Bravery and Honor the Dead_

Paladins who take the Oath of Valhalla ensure they preserve the spirits of those who have died honorably in battle, even their foes. Of course, these Paladins also strive to prove themselves deserving of entry into Valhalla through their own deeds and words. The thunderous revelry of the heroic dead echoing from the gods’ mead hall in Asgard fills these paladins, imbuing them with the might of deceased champions.

These Paladins look askance at those who restore heroes to life, but they assume such heroes have agreed to return from Valhalla to continue to fight as mortals. However, they offer no quarter to blasphemers who animate corpses into a mockery of life.

These Paladins share the following tenets.

- Protect the worthy dead.
- Inspire courage in battle.
- Bring justice to those who make a mockery of the dead.

### Level 3: Encouraging Smite

Immediately after you cast _[[Divine Smite]]_, you can expend one use of your Channel Divinity and select creatures of your choice within 30 feet of yourself. Until the start of your next turn, these creatures have [[Advantage]] on their attack rolls and saving throws against the target of your _Divine Smite_, and their attacks against the target deal an extra 1d4 Thunder damage.

### Level 3: Guardian of the Dead

As a [[Bonus Action]], you can expend one use of your Channel Divinity to better protect the dead under your watch. For 1 hour, whenever you are within 10 feet of a creature reduced to 0 Hit Points, you have [[Advantage]] on your attack rolls and on saving throws against spells and effects that inflict the [[Charmed]] or [[Frightened]] condition. After you gain your Aura of Courage class feature, you have [[Immunity]] to the Charmed condition when using this feature.

During this 1-hour period, when a creature you can see casts a spell to create Undead, or restore a creature to life, you can take a [[Reaction]] to move up to half your Speed and attack the creature that cast the spell.

### Level 3: Oath of Valhalla Spells

The magic of your oath ensures you always have certain spells ready; when you reach a Paladin level specified in the **Oath of Valhalla Spells** table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

### Level 7: Thunderous Aura

You and your allies have [[Immunity]] to Thunder damage while in your Aura of Protection.

Also, when you cast _[[Find Steed]]_, you can choose your Faithful Steed’s attacks to deal Thunder damage instead of its typical damage.

### Level 15: Valorous Soul

When you reduce a foe to 0 Hit Points with a melee attack, you can choose any number of allies within 60 feet of you. For 1 minute, these allies have [[Advantage]] on their attack rolls and saving throws.

Should you die, your body is treated as if _[[Gentle Repose]]_ had been cast on it, and if you choose, you can refuse to return to life when you are the target of _[[Raise Dead]]_ and similar spells.

### Level 20: Spirit of the Valkyrie

As a [[Bonus Action]], you receive the blessings granted to Valkyries by Wotan. For 10 minutes or until you choose to end this effect (no action required), you gain the following benefits.

**_Flight._** You have a [[Fly Speed]] of 60 feet.

**_Magic Resistance._** You have [[Advantage]] on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**_Restful Dead._** A creature within your Aura of Protection that attempts to create or summon Undead must make a Charisma saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, the attempt to create or summon Undead fails.

**_Thunderstruck._** When you cast a spell with “Smite” in its name, such as _[[Divine Smite]]_ or _[[Shining Smite]]_, you can expend any level of spell slot but cast it as though you used a 5th-level spell slot. Additionally, when an enemy starts its turn in your Aura of Protection, it takes Thunder damage equal to your Charisma modifier plus your Proficiency Bonus, and it must make a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, it has the [[Stunned]] condition until the end of its next turn.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a [[Long Rest]]. You can also restore your use of it by expending a level 5 spell slot (no action required).
