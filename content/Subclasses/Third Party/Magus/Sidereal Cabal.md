_Master Your Destiny (and Everyone Else’s Too)_

Magi in the Sidereal Cabal consult the stars to foresee the future and intercede at moments of particular import. Long ago the cabal followed a philosophy of balance, believing that destiny and free will should be given equal weight. Today the cabal is split into two camps, kismetists and volitionists. Kismetists prefer indirect and discreet methods, believing that active and obvious interference in the world’s affairs cause a disruption in the natural order of fate. Volitionists find pivotal moments where the road of destiny forks and interject themselves to force the outcome they prefer.

### Level 3: Selenite Mark

The effects of your mystic marks are enhanced by your greater understanding of the Sidereal Cabal’s esoteric knowledge. When you use your Mystic Mark feature, you give off a soft indistinct multicolored glow and hum with potential energy. You gain the following effects based on the benefit or benefits chosen.

_**Aegis.**_ When a willing creature you can see within 30 feet of you is hit by an attack, you can use your [[Reaction]] to make the attacker reroll the attack with [[Disadvantage]]. It must use the new result.

_**Enspell.**_ Once per turn when you miss a creature with a weapon or [[Unarmed Strike]], you can make the attack roll again with [[Advantage]]. You must use the new result.

### Level 3: Sidereal Cabal Magic

You can use an [[Astrolabe]] or [[Arcane Focus]] as a [[Spellcasting Focus]] for your Magus spells.

In addition, your training in the Sidereal Cabal ensures you always have certain spells ready; when you reach a Magus level specified in the Sidereal Cabal Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

### Level 3: Starlit Sight

You gain the following benefits.

_**Darkvision.**_ You have [[Darkvision]] with a range of 60 feet. If you already have Darkvision when you gain this feature, its range increases by 60 feet.

_**Mini-Portent.**_ Whenever you finish a [[Long Rest]], roll one d20 and record the number rolled. You can replace any [[D20 Test]] made by you or a creature that you can see with this foretold roll. You must choose to do so before the roll. This foretold roll can be used only once. When you finish a Long Rest, you lose any unused foretold roll.

_**Revelator’s Eyes.**_ You gain proficiency in the [[Perception]] skill. If you are already proficient with Perception, you gain [[Expertise]] in it instead.

### Level 7: Celestial Clockwork Control

Your extensive study of the stars has taught you to twist the heavenly mechanics of fate in your favor. After a creature you can see within 60 feet of you makes a [[D20 Test]] you can expend a spell slot to modify the result of the roll. When you do, you can choose to increase or decrease the result of the roll by the level of the expended spell slot, potentially turning a failure into a success or vice versa.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until the start of your next turn.

### Level 14: Seal Fate

When a creature that you can see within 60 feet makes a roll with [[Advantage]] or [[Disadvantage]], you can seal its fate. The creature rolls three d20s, rather than two, using the highest result if it had Advantage or the lowest result if it had Disadvantage.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a [[Short Rest|Short]] or [[Long Rest]].

### Level 20: Starfinder

You gain the following benefits.

_**Selenite Spell Surge.**_ You can expend the use of your Spell Surge to cast _[[First Speech]]_ or _[[True Seeing]]_.

_**Weave Destiny.**_ You can use your Seal Fate three times, regaining one use when you finish a [[Short Rest]] and all uses when you finish a [[Long Rest]].
