_Fight for Control from the Shadows_

Although outsiders see the Shadow Court as a singular group, it is in fact multiple clans that wage clandestine wars against one another to obtain secrets and access to resources. Magi in the Shadow Court specialize in legerdemain, stealth, and assassination. Using these tools, these Magi manipulate political powers to stymie their enemies and advance their own agendas.

### Level 3: Hand and Foot Training

You gain the following benefits.

_**Covert Warrior.**_ You gain proficiency in the [[Stealth]] skill and with [[Thieves' Tools]]. If you are already proficient with Stealth, you gain [[Expertise]] in it instead.

_**Enshadowed Equipment.**_ You can take a [[Bonus Action]] to cause one of the following objects to magically coalesce from shadows in your hands: [[Caltrops]], [[Costume]], [[Crowbar]], [[Grappling Hook]], [[Ladder]], [[Net]], [[Parchment]], [[Rope]], [[Signal Whistle]], [[Spyglass]], or [[Thieves' Tools]]. This item remains until it has been exposed to [[Bright Light]] for 10 minutes, you dismiss it (no action required), or you use this Bonus Action again.

You can use this Bonus Action a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Long Rest]].

_**Martial Artist.**_ You can use your Dexterity modifier instead of your Strength modifier for the attack and damage rolls of your [[Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strikes]], and you can roll 1d6 in place of the normal damage of your Unarmed Strike. In addition, when you use the Grapple or Shove option of your Unarmed Strike, you can use your Dexterity modifier instead of your Strength modifier to determine the save DC.

### Level 3: Jade Mark

The effects of your mystic marks are enhanced by your greater understanding of the Shadow Court’s esoteric knowledge. When you use your Mystic Mark feature, you are engulfed in an aura of translucent smoke and you gain the following effects based on the benefit or benefits chosen.

_**Aegis.**_ When a creature you can see within 30 feet of you is hit by an attack, you can use your [[Reaction]] to give the creature the [[Invisible]] condition until the end of its next turn. The condition ends early if the target makes an attack roll, deals damage, or casts a spell.

_**Enspell.**_ Once per turn when you hit a creature with a weapon or [[Unarmed Strike]], you can make the target overexposed until the start of your next turn or until it is hit with a [[Critical Hit]]. While overexposed, attack rolls against the target can score a Critical Hit on a roll of 19 or 20.

### Level 3: Shadow Court Magic

You can use [[Parchment]] as a [[Spellcasting Focus]] for your Magus spells. When you do, if the spell is level 1+ the Parchment is destroyed.

In addition, your training in the Shadow Court ensures you always have certain spells ready; when you reach a Magus level specified in the Shadow Court Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

### Level 7: Broken Mirror Jutsu

You always have the _[[Mirror Image]]_ spell prepared.

You can use a [[Bonus Action]] to cast _Mirror Image_ without expending a spell slot. When you do, each time a duplicate is destroyed the attacking creature must succeed on an Intelligence saving throw against your Magus spell save DC or take Psychic damage equal to your Magus level plus your Intelligence modifier.

Once you use this Bonus Action, you can’t use it again until you finish a [[Long Rest]] unless you expend a level 2+ spell slot (no action required) to restore your use of it.

### Level 14: False Body Jutsu

You can replace yourself with an unlikely body double in the instant before you take a blow. When you are hit by an attack, you can take a [[Reaction]] to reduce the damage from that attack to 1. When you do, you have the [[Invisible]] condition for 1 minute and teleport to an unoccupied space of your choice within 300 feet of you. You leave a block of wood or another harmless, mundane object in the space you teleported away from.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a [[Long Rest]].

### Level 20: Enshadowed Sifu

You gain the following benefits.

_**Jade Spell Surge.**_ You can expend the use of your Spell Surge to cast _[[Eyebite]]_ or _[[Programmed Illusion]]_.

_**Shadow Puppeteer.**_ When you finish a [[Short Rest]], you can regain the use of your False Body Jutsu. Once you regain the use of it in this way, you can’t do so again until you finish a [[Long Rest]].
