_Make Heaven a Place on Earth_

Legend has it that in a time before recorded history, an elite host of Celestial warriors forsook their immortality and the divine realms, becoming mortal to protect the holy people and places of the Material Plane. The story goes that these martyrs’ legacy lives on in the Heaven’s Guard, an order of Magi that act as bodyguards to the righteous and sentinels of hallowed temples. Whether or not such tales are true, Magi in the Heaven’s Guard eagerly serve as living shields to their chosen charges by drawing their enemies’ ire and healing what wounds their adversaries manage to inflict.

### Level 3: Alabaster Mark

The effects of your mystic marks are enhanced by your greater understanding of the Heaven’s Guard’s esoteric knowledge. When you use your Mystic Mark feature, a soft white and gold aura envelops you, and you gain the following effects based on the benefit or benefits chosen.

_**Aegis.**_ When a willing creature you can see within 30 feet of you is hit by an attack, you can use your [[Reaction]] to guard the creature until the start of your next turn. While the creature is guarded this way, attacks against it have [[Disadvantage]].

_**Enspell.**_ Once per turn when you hit a creature with a weapon or [[Unarmed Strike]], it has [[Disadvantage]] on attacks against creatures other than you until the start of your next turn.

### Level 3: Heaven’s Guard Magic

You can use a [[Holy Symbol]] as a [[Spellcasting Focus]] for your Magus spells.

In addition, your training in the Heaven’s Guard ensures you always have certain spells ready; when you reach a Magus level specified in the Heaven’s Guard Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

### Level 3: Heavensent

You gain the following benefits.

_**Best Offense.**_ While you are using a [[Shield]], you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls with weapons and [[Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strikes]].

_**Gifted Healer.**_ When you cause a creature to regain Hit Points with a spell or Magus feature, you can expend a [[Hit Point Dice|Hit Die]], rolling it and adding the result to the total number of Hit Points restored.

_**Heaven’s Hand.**_ When you cast a Magus spell that deals damage, it can deal your choice of Radiant damage or its normal damage type.

### Level 7: Guardian’s Wings

As a [[Bonus Action]], you can fly up to twice your Speed. When you do, at the end of your turn creatures of your choice within 10 feet of you regain Hit Points equal to your Magus level plus your Intelligence modifier.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a [[Short Rest|Short]] or [[Long Rest]].

### Level 14: Cloak of Radiance

As a [[Bonus Action]] you can cloak another creature within 30 feet of you and that you can see in radiant light. The target gains [[Temporary Hit Points]] equal to 5d10 plus your Magus level. While a creature has any Temporary Hit Points granted by this feature, it has the benefits listed below. Once you have used this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a [[Long Rest]].

_**Brilliant.**_ Creatures that do not have [[Immunity]] to the [[Blinded]] condition have [[Disadvantage]] on attacks against the target.

_**Bright.**_ The target sheds [[Bright Light]] out to 30 feet and [[Dim Light]] for an additional 30 feet.

_**Buoyant.**_ The target can take a Bonus Action on each of its turns to Fly up to 30 feet. If the creature does not land at the end of this movement, it falls to the ground without taking damage.

### Level 20: Bright as Dawn

You gain the following benefits.

_**Alabaster Spell Surge.**_ You can expend the use of your Spell Surge to cast _[[Heal]]_ or _[[Sunbeam]]_.

_**Celestial Shieldbearer.**_ When you finish a [[Short Rest]], you can regain the use of your Cloak of Radiance. Once you regain the use of it in this way, you can’t do so again until you finish a [[Long Rest]].
