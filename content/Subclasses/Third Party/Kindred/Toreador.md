_See the World Like No One Else Can_

Toreador are steeped in art and beauty. They use their superhuman senses to experience aspects of the world no one else can. They feel everything deeply, and, with a glance or a few words, can instill similar feelings in others. If there is awe to be found, a Toreador can find it.

### Level 3: An Artist’s Soul

You have in you a spark of talent and inspiration that has only been fanned brighter by your vampiric ascension. You gain the following benefits.

**_Auspicious Senses._** You have [[Advantage]] on Intelligence ([[Investigation]]) and Wisdom ([[Perception]]) checks. In addition, the range of your [[Darkvision]] increases to 120 feet.

**_Chosen for Talent._** You gain proficiency in two skills and one tool of your choice. If you already have proficiency in the chosen skill, you gain [[Expertise]] in it.

### Level 3: Toreador Bane

You suffer a great malaise in the absence of beauty. When you roll a 9 or lower on the d20 for an Intelligence ([[Investigation]]) or Wisdom ([[Perception]]) check, you see nothing of immediate beauty and gain the [[Restrained]] condition. At the end of each of your turns, you can make a DC 10 Wisdom saving throw, ending the condition on a success.

### Level 6: Depth of Feelings

You can see aspects of the world that others can’t, including emotions. You gain the following benefits.

**_Aura Sight._** As a [[Magic]] action, you can expend 2 Blood Points to see a creature’s aura for 1 minute. You can ask the GM up to two questions that can be answered with yes or no. You must ask your questions before the effect ends. You receive a correct answer for each question. The following are example questions.

- Is the creature’s creature type X? (Where X is a creature type specified by the player character, such as Monstrosity or Undead)
- Can the creature cast spells?
- Is the creature [[Hostile]] toward me?
- Does the creature believe what it is saying?

The GM has final say as to whether the question can be answered, but the answers must be truthful.

**_Entrancement._** You can expend 1 Blood Point to cast the _[[Calm Emotions]]_, _[[Charm Monster]]_, or _[[Charm Person]]_ spell.

### Level 9: Live Fast, Be a Good Looking Corpse

You experience the world, in all its wonders, with vampiric speed. You gain the following benefits.

**_Ability Score Increase._** Your Dexterity score increases by 2, to a maximum of 25.

**_Rapidity._** As a [[Bonus Action]], you can expend 1 Blood Point to gain an additional action on your turns. That action can be used to take only the [[Attack]] (one attack only), [[Dash]], [[Disengage]], [[Hide]], or [[Utilize]] action. This effects lasts for a number of turns equal to your Proficiency Bonus.

### Level 11: Visionary

You have honed your skills and your perceptions to a razor’s edge. You gain the following benefits.

**_Practiced Perfection._** You gain [[Expertise]] in three of your skill proficiencies of your choice.

**_Spirit’s Touch._** As a [[Magic]] action, you can expend 2 Blood Points to touch an object and perceive emotional residues on it for 10 minutes. You can ask the GM a number of questions equal to your Proficiency Bonus, which must be answered truthfully. You must ask your questions before the effect ends. The following are examples of questions that can be asked.

- What did the last creature that touched this look like?
- What was the emotional state of the last creature that used the object?
- Is this object magical?
- Is this object cursed?
- Is this object trapped?
- What are any physical details of the object that aren’t readily visible?
- How many creatures have touched the object in the last 24 hours?

### Level 15: Truly Majestic

You exude vampiric allure and have learned how to charm others. You gain the following benefits.

**_Eerily Convincing._** You have [[Advantage]] on Charisma ([[Deception]] and [[Persuasion]]) checks. In addition, when you cast a spell with your Entrancement feature, a creature has [[Disadvantage]] on any saving throw it makes against the spell.

**_Majesty._** You can expend 3 Blood Points to cast the _[[Sanctuary]]_ spell on yourself. When you cast the spell in this way, the spell doesn’t end if you make an attack roll, cast a spell, or deal damage.

### Level 18: Magnum Opus

You have mastered what it means to be one of the greatest vampiric artisans, which defines you as much as you define it. Choose two of the following benefits.

**_Flicker._** As a [[Bonus Action]], you can expend 3 Blood Points to dance between the beats of a mortal’s heart. Until the start of your next turn, you have a +3 bonus to AC, your Speed is doubled, your movement doesn’t provoke [[Opportunity Attacks]], and you have [[Advantage]] on attack rolls.

**_Clairvoyance._** You can expend 2 Blood Points to cast the _[[Clairvoyance]]_ or _[[Scrying]]_ spell. When you cast the spell in this way, the sensor can’t be perceived.

**_Open Mind._** You can use a [[Magic]] action to expend 1 Blood Point to choose any number of creatures you know within 5 miles of yourself or any number of creatures you can see within 120 feet of yourself. You and the chosen creatures can communicate telepathically with each other while the creatures and you are within 5 miles of each other for 8 hours. You and the targets can instantly share words, images, sounds, and other sensory messages with each other through the link, and the targets recognize you as the creature they are communicating with. This effect enables creatures to understand the meaning of your words and any sensory messages you send to them.

**_Star Magnetism._** You can expend 3 Blood Points to cast the _[[Mass Suggestion]]_ spell. When you cast the spell in this way, you can target any creature you can communicate with, you don’t need line of sight, the range becomes 1 mile, and you can modify it so that it doesn’t require [[Concentration]].
