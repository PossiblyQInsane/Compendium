_Let the Beast Inside in Order to Control It_

The Gangrel have made peace with their Beast long ago, choosing to harness the wild power it promises and wield it rather than trying to stifle it. They are attuned to its feral nature, embodying the predator.

### Level 3: Gangrel Bane

The Beast reveals itself in you as animalistic features and behaviors. If you are affected by the Hunger of your Beast feature, you have [[Disadvantage]] on Charisma ([[Deception]] and [[Persuasion]]) checks until you finish a [[Long Rest]].

### Level 3: Gifts of the Beast

Your body reflects the protean gifts of your animalistic Beast. You gain the following benefits.

**_Eyes of the Beast._** The range of your [[Darkvision]] increases to 240 feet.

**_Feral Weapons._** As a [[Bonus Action]], you can expend 1 Blood Point to grow rending claws for 1 minute. Your [[Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strikes]] deal 2d6 Slashing damage, and you can use your Dexterity modifier instead of your Strength modifier for the attack and damage rolls of your Unarmed Strikes.

### Level 6: Feral Spirit

Your connection to your wild self forges a spiritual bridge with Beasts.

**_Beastmind._** While you look in the eyes of a Beast within 30 feet of yourself, you can speak telepathically with it, and you can use any of the [[Influence]] action’s skill options with it.

**_Like Minds._** You can cast the _[[Animal Friendship]]_ spell without expending a spell slot. And when you cast the spell in this way, it is cast as if you had used a spell slot level equal to your Proficiency Bonus.

**_Wild Hunger._** When you are affected by the Hunger of your Beast feature, you can deal 1d6 extra Slashing damage when you hit it with an [[Unarmed Strike]], and you also count as one size larger when determining what you can grapple.

### Level 9: Gifts of Survival

Your body is built to survive whatever the world sets before you.

**_Feral Fortitude._** When you take damage except Fire and Radiant, you can take a [[Reaction]] to reduce the damage taken by your Proficiency Bonus plus your Constitution modifier (minimum reduction of 1).

**_Meld with Earth._** As a [[Magic]] action, you can expend 1 Blood Point to cast the _[[Meld into Stone]]_ spell, except the surface must be dirt, sand, or clay instead of stone. Additionally, while the spell is active, you gain [[Tremorsense]] with a range of 60 feet. The spell lasts until you end it (no action required) or when you finish a [[Long Rest]].

### Level 11: Corporeal Mastery

You embody the supernatural powers of ancient vampire legends, transforming for a time.

**_Beast Form_**. As a [[Magic]] action, you can expend a number of Blood Points to shape-shift into a Beast form applicable to this feature (see “Known Forms” below). You stay in that form for a number of hours equal to half your Kindred level or until you use Corporeal Mastery again, have the [[Incapacitated]] condition, or die. You can also leave the form early as a [[Bonus Action]].

**_Known Forms._** You can expend 2 Blood Points to shape-shift into a **[[Bat]]**, **[[Cat]]**, **[[Rat]]**, **[[Raven]]**, or **[[Wolf]]**. You can expend 4 Blood Points to shape-shift into a **[[Dire Wolf]]**, **[[Giant Bat]]**, **[[Giant Rat]]**, or **[[Panther]]**.

**_Temporary Hit Points._** When you assume a Beast form, you gain a number of [[Temporary Hit Points]] equal to your Kindred level.

**_Game Statistics._** Your game statistics are replaced by the Beast’s stat block, but you retain your creature type; Hit Points; [[Hit Point Dice]]; Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma scores; class features; languages; and feats. You also retain your skill and saving throw proficiencies and use your Proficiency Bonus for them, in addition to gaining the proficiencies of the creature. If a skill or saving throw modifier in the Beast’s stat block is higher than yours, use the one in the stat block. While in Beast form, you retain your personality and memories, and you can understand languages you know, but you can’t speak.

**_Objects._** Your ability to handle objects is determined by the form’s limbs rather than your own. In addition, your equipment merges into your new form. Nonmagical equipment that merges with the form has no effect while you’re in that form.

### Level 15: Command the Beast

You can impose your will upon wild things, allowing your Beast to overwhelm theirs.

**_Rein._** You cast the _[[Dominate Beast]]_ spell as its level 7 version, without expending a spell slot, and you can modify it so that it doesn’t require [[Concentration]].

Once you cast the spell with this feature, you can’t do so in this way again until you finish a [[Long Rest]].

**_Rouse._** When an Undead (Kindred) you can see within 30 feet of you is about to make a [[D20 Test]], you can take a [[Reaction]] and expend 1 Blood Point to force that creature to use its Beast feature on that D20 Test. If the Kindred has no remaining uses of its Beast feature, this effect automatically fails.

### Level 18: Protean Rewards

Your body can perform greater wonders.

**_Flesh of Marble._** As a [[Magic]] action, you can expend 2 Blood Points to double your Gifts of Survival feature’s Damage Reduction for 1 minute.

**_Mistform._** You can expend 2 Blood Points to cast the _[[Gaseous Form]]_ spell on yourself. When you cast the spell in this way, you can modify it so that it doesn’t require [[Concentration]].
