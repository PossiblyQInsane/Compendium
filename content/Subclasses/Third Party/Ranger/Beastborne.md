_Channel Lycanthropic Fury_

Whether by infected bite or insidious curse, your blood was infected with the magic of lycanthropy. A ferocious, bestial form lies just beneath your skin, awaiting the allure of blood and the thrill of a hunt. Perhaps, if you can avoid the violence or the siren call of moonlight, you can resist the lycanthropic urge. Or perhaps you might embrace the infection as a blessing or wield it as a fearsome weapon.

### Level 3: Lycanthrope

Your true form is a bestial hybrid, granting you the following benefits.

**_Claws._** You have a set of vicious claws that you can use to make [[Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strikes]]. You can use your Dexterity modifier instead of your Strength modifier for the attack and damage rolls of your Unarmed Strikes. When you deal damage with an Unarmed Strike, you can deal Slashing damage equal to 1d6 plus your Strength or Dexterity modifier instead of the strike’s normal damage.

**_Climb Speed._** You gain a [[Climb Speed]] equal to your Speed.

**_Darkvision._** You gain [[Darkvision]] with a range of 60 feet. If you already have Darkvision, its range increases by 60 feet.

### Level 3: Bestial Aspect

In the throes of combat, you gradually lose control and slip into a beastlike form. The degree of your transformation is represented by your Bestial Aspect Level, which starts at 0. Your Bestial Aspect Level grants you benefits, as shown on the Bestial Aspect table. You gain all the benefits of your Bestial Aspect Level and lower.

When you deal damage to an enemy, you can take a [[Bonus Action]] to increase your Bestial Aspect Level by 1. If you don’t deal damage to an enemy for 1 minute, your Bestial Aspect Level resets to 0.

### Level 7: Feral Howl

When you roll Initiative, you can roll a d4 and increase your Bestial Aspect Level to the number rolled.

### Level 11: Bloodthirsty Fury

You have gained control over your bestial rage, granting you the following benefits.

**_Bestial Mark._** You can cast _[[Hunter's Mark]]_ as a part of the [[Bonus Action]] to increase your Bestial Aspect Level.

**_Greater Carnage._** The damage bonus of your Carnage benefit increases to +3.

### Level 15: Monstrous Resilience

Once per turn when you take damage, you can reduce the total damage you take, to a minimum of 0. The reduction equals your Constitution modifier plus your Bestial Aspect Level.
