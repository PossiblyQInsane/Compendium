_Wield Swords and Sorcery as a Reincarnated Hero_

You are the reincarnation of a legendary hero known to have slain many terrible foes, and your sorcerous magic draws from your legendary past. Being called back to life, whether by the gods, a very powerful mage, or a cycle of reincarnation, has awakened a well of magic and your ancient instincts for battle.

### Level 3: Martial Sorcery

Your past heroic life grants you the following benefits.

**_Heroic Soul._** At the start of each of your turns, you can spend 1 Sorcery Point to gain [[Temporary Hit Points]] equal to 1d6 plus your Sorcerer level (no action required).

**_Innate Bladework._** Whenever you attack with a weapon with which you have proficiency while your Innate Sorcery feature is active, you can use your Charisma modifier for the attack and damage rolls instead of using Strength or Dexterity.

**_Martial Training._** You gain proficiency with Martial weapons and training with Light armor, Medium armor, and Shields.

### Level 6: Extra Attack

You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the [[Attack]] action on your turn. Moreover, you can cast one of your Sorcerer cantrips that has a casting time of an action in place of one of those attacks.

### Level 14: Mystical Maneuvers

You can use the following martial maneuvers.

**_Blinding Attack._** When you hit a creature with an attack using a weapon or [[Unarmed Strike]], you can expend two Sorcery Points as a [[Bonus Action]] to attempt to blind the target. Add 2d8 to the attack’s damage roll. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC or have the [[Blinded]] condition for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the condition on itself on a success.

**_Ruinous Blow._** When you hit a creature with an attack using a weapon or [[Unarmed Strike]], you can expend two Sorcery Points as a Bonus Action to sunder its defenses. Add 2d8 to the attack’s damage roll. The target has a −3 penalty to AC until the end of your next turn.

**_Wounding Strike._** When you hit a creature with an attack using a weapon or Unarmed Strike, you can expend two Sorcery Points as a Bonus Action to deal the target a bleeding wound. Add 2d8 to the attack’s damage roll. A creature with a bleeding wound can’t regain Hit Points and takes Necrotic damage equal to 1d8 for each of its wounds at the start of each of its turns. This damage ignores [[Resistance]] and [[Immunity]]. A creature can take an action to staunch all of its bleeding wounds.

### Level 18: Heroic Haste

Whenever you start casting _[[Haste]]_ targeting yourself, you can modify it so that it doesn’t require [[Concentration]]. If you do so, when the spell ends, you don’t have the [[Incapacitated]] condition or a Speed of 0.
