_Craft a Mighty Weapon out of Your Soul_

The daashor artificers of the Dhakaani Empire were the finest weaponsmiths in Khorvaire’s history. As a Forge Adept, you carry on these ancient traditions, creating mystical weapons and armor, strengthening allies on the battlefield, and bringing down enemies with weapons imbued with your own soul energy. If you’re dar, you learned these techniques from the masters of your clan. Are you content to follow ancient traditions, or do you want to add your own innovations? If you’re not Dhakaani, did you learn these techniques from a daashor mentor? Did you steal this knowledge? Or are you replicating these traditions on your own?

### Level 3: Tool Proficiency

You gain proficiency with [[Smith's Tools]]. If you already have this proficiency, you gain proficiency with one other type of [[Artisan's Tools]] of your choice.

In addition, when you [[Crafting Equipment|craft]] an ordinary or magic weapon, the amount of time required to craft it is halved.

### Level 3: Forge Adept Spells

When you reach an Artificer level specified in the Forge Adept Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

### Level 3: Battle Ready

Your combat training and your experiments with magic have paid off in two ways:

_**Arcane Empowerment.**_ When you attack with a magic weapon, you can use your Intelligence modifier, instead of Strength or Dexterity, for the attack and damage rolls.

_**Weapon Knowledge.**_ You gain proficiency with [[Martial weapons]]. You can use a weapon with which you have proficiency as a [[Spellcasting Focus]] for your Artificer spells.

### Level 3: Ghaal'Shaarat

You can imbue a weapon with a fraction of your spirit, creating what the Dhakaani daashors call a ghaal’shaarat, Goblin for “mighty blade.” As a Magic action while you have [[Smith's Tools]] in hand, you can turn a Simple or Martial Melee weapon into a Ghaal’Shaarat. The weapon continues to be a Ghaal’Shaarat until you imbue a different weapon or you die.

The weapon gains the following benefits while you wield it.

_**Mighty Blade.**_ A nonmagical weapon becomes a magic weapon. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with the weapon, unless it already had this bonus or a better one.

_**Returning Weapon.**_ The weapon has the [[Thrown]] property with a normal range of 30 feet and a long range of 120 feet. It returns to your hand immediately after it is used to make a ranged attack roll.

### Level 5: Extra Attack

You can attack twice instead of once whenever you take the [[Attack]] action on your turn.

### Level 9: Runes of War

You gain the following benefits while wielding your Ghaal’Shaarat.

_**Improved Mighty Blade.**_ You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with the weapon, unless it already had this bonus or a better one.

_**Aura of War.**_ As a [[Magic]] action, you begin radiating a bolstering aura in a 30-foot [[Emanation]] that originates from you. Choose one of the following damage types: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder. While in the aura, you and your allies each deal an extra 1d4 damage of the chosen type when hitting with a weapon attack or [[Unarmed Strike]]. The aura lasts for 1 minute, until you use this feature again, or until you end it (no action required). You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of once). You regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Long Rest]].

### Level 15: Perfect Weapon

The connection of your spirit to your weapon has been perfected. You gain the following benefits while wielding your Ghaal’Shaarat.

_**Perfect Mighty Blade.**_ You gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with the weapon, unless it already had this bonus or a better one. Additionally, the first time you attack with your Ghaal’Shaarat on your turn, you can transfer some or all of the weapon’s bonus to your [[Armor Class]]. The bonus remains until the start of your next turn.

_**Elemental Strike.**_ When you hit a creature with an attack roll using your Ghaal’Shaarat, choose one of the following damage types: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder. Your Ghaal’Shaarat deals an extra 1d6 damage of the chosen type; and you can cause the weapon to deal the chosen type of damage or its normal damage type.

_**Soul Bound.**_ You have [[Resistance]] to Psychic damage, and you have [[Immunity]] to the [[Charmed]] and [[Frightened]] conditions.

> ### Level 20: Weapon of Legend
>
> You are able to connect your spirit to your allies, making them living weapons. You gain the following benefits while wielding your Ghaal’Shaarat.
>
> _**Constant Aura.**_ Your Aura of War is always active, and you can take a [[Bonus Action]] to change its damage type.
>
> _**Retributive Aura.**_ Whenever an enemy starts its turn in your Aura of War, that creature takes damage equal to the extra damage provided by the aura.
>
> _**Empower Aura.**_ As a [[Bonus Action]], you can increase the extra damage provided by your Aura of War to 2d6. This increase lasts for 1 minute. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of once). You regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Long Rest]].
