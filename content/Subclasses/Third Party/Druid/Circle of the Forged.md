_Shape-shift into Beasts of Steel and Wood_

What is truly natural? The warforged are living creatures, despite being constructed from wood and steel. Druids who embrace the Circle of the Forged explore the potential of the warforged form, blending animal shapes with warforged durability. Only a handful of Druids follow this Circle. Did you learn these techniques from a mentor? Are you driven by instinct, still learning what you’re capable of? Or, if you’re a Warforged, were you designed with the potential to assume multiple forms... and if so, who created you, and why?

### Level 3: Skin of Steel

You can channel the power of the forge when you assume a Wild Shape form, granting you the benefits below.

_**Challenge Rating.**_ The maximum Challenge Rating for the form equals your Druid level divided by 3 (round down).

_**Armor Class.**_ Until you leave the form, your AC equals 15 plus your Wisdom modifier if that total is higher than the Beast’s AC.

_**Construct Resilience.**_ You have [[Resistance]] to Poison damage. You also have [[Advantage]] on saving throws to avoid or end the [[Poisoned]] condition.

_**Tireless.**_ You don’t gain [[Exhaustion]] levels from [[Dehydration|dehydration]], [[Malnutrition|malnutrition]], or [[Suffocation|suffocation]]. In addition, you don’t need to sleep, and magic can’t put you to sleep.

_**Obvious.**_ While in a Wild Shape form, your body is made from the same materials as a warforged; your muscles are rootlike tendrils, and they’re protected by armored plates. It’s obvious to an observer that you are not a normal animal.

### Level 6: Elementally Imbued

While in a Wild Shape form, you gain the following benefits.

_**Elemental Strikes.**_ Each of your attacks in a Wild Shape form can deal its normal damage type, Acid damage, Cold damage, Fire damage, or Lightning damage. You make this choice each time you hit with those attacks.

_**Elemental Cascade.**_ Choose one of the following damage types: Acid, Cold, Fire, or Lightning. Once per turn when you hit with a Wild Shape form’s attack, you can expend a spell slot (no action required) to deal extra damage of the chosen type equal to 1d6 per level of the spell slot, to a maximum of 5d6. The target must make a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, the target suffers an effect based on the chosen damage type for 1 minute. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

**Acid.** The target suffers a penalty to AC equal to half your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1).

**Cold.** The target’s [[Speed]] is 0 and they have [[Disadvantage]] on Strength and Dexterity checks.

**Fire.** At the start of each of its turns, the target takes Fire damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.

**Lightning.** The target can’t make [[Opportunity Attacks]], and the first attack it makes on each of its turns has [[Disadvantage]].

### Level 10: Adamantine Hide

While in a Wild Shape form, you gain [[Resistance]] to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.

In addition, when you are hit by an attack roll or forced to make a saving throw, you can take a [[Reaction]] and expend a use of Wild Shape to shape-shift into one of your Wild Shape forms.

### Level 14: Construction Perfection

While in a Wild Shape form, you gain the following additional benefits.

_**Armor Class.**_ Until you leave the form, your AC equals 16 plus your Wisdom modifier if that total is higher than the Beast’s AC.

_**Iron Mind.**_ You have [[Immunity]] to the [[Charmed]], [[Frightened]], [[Paralyzed]], [[Petrified]], and [[Poisoned]] conditions, and Poison damage.
