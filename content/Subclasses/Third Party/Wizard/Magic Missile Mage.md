_Cast Magic Missile Better Than Anyone_

_[[Magic Missile]]_ is a staple wizard spell, beloved for its versatility and unerring accuracy. As a Missile Mage, however, your fondness for the spell borders on obsession. Your studies focus entirely on this spell, exploring every aspect of its casting, resulting in new augmentations and greater power.

### Level 3: Magic Missile Savant

Add the _[[Magic Missile]]_ spell to your spellbook for free. Your expertise in this spell grants you the following benefits.

**_Extra Darts._** When you cast _Magic Missile_, you create an extra dart of magical force. You create two extra darts at Wizard level 6, three extra darts at Wizard level 10, and four extra darts at Wizard level 14.

**_Free Casting._** You can cast _Magic Missile_ without a spell slot. You can do so a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of once). You regain one expended use when you finish a [[Short Rest]], and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Long Rest]].

**_Penetrating Darts._** Your _Magic Missile_ darts bypass any effect, such as the _[[Shield (Spell)|Shield]]_ spell, that specifically blocks the _Magic Missile_ spell.

### Level 6: Versatile Missiles

You can accurately guide your _[[Magic Missile]]_ darts to strike a target wherever you choose. When you cast _Magic Missile_, you can add one of the following Versatile Missile effects to a target struck by the darts. Each effect has a cost, which is the number of darts you must forgo to add the effect to the target. You can add different effects to different targets, paying the cost for each effect separately.

**_Trip Missile (Cost: 1 Dart)._** The target must succeed on Strength saving throw against your spell save DC or have the [[Prone]] condition.

**_Blinding Missile (Cost: 3 Darts)._** The target must succeed on Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC or have the [[Blinded]] condition until the start of your next turn.

**_Stunning Missile (Cost: 5 Darts)._** The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC or have the [[Stunned]] condition until the start of your next turn.

### Level 10: Shield of Missiles

When you cast _[[Magic Missile]]_, you can direct the darts in a tight orbit around yourself, creating a protective 10-foot [[Emanation]] originating from you, which lasts for up to 1 minute.

You gain a bonus to your Armor Class equal to the number of darts orbiting you (maximum bonus of +5). Whenever a creature within 120 feet misses you with an attack roll, one dart strikes the attacker, dealing damage as normal and decreasing the number of darts orbiting you by one.

Whenever the Emanation enters the space of a creature you can see and whenever a creature you can see enters the Emanation or ends its turn there, you can strike the target with any number of darts orbiting you, dealing damage as normal and decreasing the number of darts orbiting you. You can damage a creature with the Emanation only once per turn.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a [[Long Rest]]. You can also restore your use of it by expending a level 3+ spell slot (no action required).

### Level 14: Giga-Missile

When you cast _[[Magic Missile]]_, you can enhance the damage of the spell. You deal extra Force damage with each dart equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of 1).

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a [[Long Rest]]. You can also restore your use of it by expending a level 6+ spell slot (no action required).
