_Epic Boon Feat (Prerequisite: Level 19+)_

---

You gain the following benefits.

_**Ability Score Increase.**_ Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

_**Improve Fate.**_ When you or another creature within 60 feet of you succeeds on or fails a [[D20 Test]], you can roll 2d4 and apply the total rolled as a bonus or penalty to the d20 roll. Once you use this benefit, you can’t use it again until you roll [[Initiative]] or finish a [[Short Rest|Short]] or [[Long Rest]].
