_Epic Boon Feat (Prerequisite: Level 19+, Spellcasting Feature)_

---

You gain the following benefits.

_**Ability Score Increase.**_ Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 30.

_**Free Casting.**_ Whenever you cast a spell with a level 1–4 spell slot, roll 1d4. If the number you roll is the same as the slot’s level, the slot isn’t expended.
