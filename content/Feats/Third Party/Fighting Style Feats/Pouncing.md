_Fighting Style Feat_ _(Prerequisite: Fighting Style Feature)_

---

Whenever you move 15 feet or more toward a creature immediately before making an attack against it with a Melee weapon or an [[Unarmed Strike]], you gain a +2 bonus to the attack roll. If you instead use the Grapple or Shove option of your Unarmed Strike against the creature, the DC for the saving throw it makes increases by 2.
