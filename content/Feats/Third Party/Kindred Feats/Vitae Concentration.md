_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Kindred Class)_

---

Your body is more efficient at holding and processing vitae. You gain the following benefits.

**_Ability Score Increase._** Your Constitution score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**_Increase Blood Pool._** Your Blood Point maximum increases by your Constitution modifier (minimum of 1).
