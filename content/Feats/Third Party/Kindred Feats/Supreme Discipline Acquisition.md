_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 16+, [[Superior Discipline Acquisition]] Feat)_

---

You have learned all you can about a vampiric Discipline of another clan or bloodline. Gain one of the following benefits for which you already possess the matching Discipline from the [[Superior Discipline Acquisition]] feat.

**Animalism.** You cast the _[[Dominate Beast]]_ spell as its level 7 version without expending a spell slot, and you can modify it so that it doesn’t require [[Concentration]]. Once you cast the spell with this feat, you can’t do so in this way again until you finish a [[Long Rest]].

**Auspex.** You can expend 2 Blood Points to cast the _[[True Seeing]]_ spell on yourself.

**Celerity.** You can expend 2 Blood Points to cast the _[[Haste]]_ spell on yourself. When you cast the spell in this way, when the spell ends, you aren’t [[Incapacitated]] and your Speed isn’t reduced to 0.

**Dominate.** You cast the _[[Dominate Person]]_ spell as its level 7 version without expending a spell slot, and you can modify it so that it doesn’t require [[Concentration]]. Once you cast the spell with this feat, you can’t do so in this way again until you finish a [[Long Rest]].

**Fortitude.** When you take damage except Fire and Radiant, you can take a [[Reaction]] and expend 2 Blood Points to reduce the damage you take by half (round down) or 4 Blood Points to reduce the damage to 0.

**Obfuscate.** You can expend 1 Blood Point to cast the _[[Greater Invisibility]]_ spell. When you cast the spell in this way, you can modify it so that it doesn’t require [[Concentration]]. In addition, you have [[Advantage]] on Dexterity ([[Stealth]]) checks.

**Oblivion.** When a creature that you can see within 10 feet of you misses an attack roll, you can take a [[Reaction]] and expend 1 Blood Point to deal 2d8 Bludgeoning damage and 2d8 Cold damage to that creature.

**Potence.** As a [[Bonus Action]], you can expend 2 Blood Points to give yourself [[Advantage]] on attack rolls using Strength for 1 minute. In addition, whenever you have Advantage on an attack roll using Strength, you can reroll one of the dice once.

**Presence.** You can expend 3 Blood Points to cast the _[[Sanctuary]]_ spell on yourself. When you cast the spell in this way, the spell doesn’t end if you make an attack roll, cast a spell, or deal damage.

**Protean.** You can expend 3 Blood Points to cast _[[Polymorph]]_ on yourself, shape-shifting into a **[[Bat]]**, **[[Cat]]**, **[[Rat]]**, **[[Raven]]**, or **[[Wolf]]**. You can expend 1 additional Blood Point to shape-shift into a **[[Dire Wolf]]**, **[[Giant Bat]]**, **[[Giant Rat]]**, or **[[Panther]]**. While in that form, you retain your Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma scores.

**_Repeatable._** You can take this feat more than once, but you must choose a different Discipline each time.
