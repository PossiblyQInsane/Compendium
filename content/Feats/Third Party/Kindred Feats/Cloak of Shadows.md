_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 2+, Kindred Class)_

---

You are adept at supernaturally masking your presence. You gain the following benefits.

**_Enshrouded._** As a [[Bonus Action]], you give yourself [[Advantage]] on your next Dexterity ([[Stealth]]) check on the current turn.

**_Unseen Passage._** As a [[Magic]] action, you gain the [[Invisible]] condition until the end of your next turn.

You can use this Magic action a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Long Rest]].
