_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 7+, Kindred Class)_

Your supernatural powers of persuasion are uncannily hidden from your victim’s mind. You gain the following benefits.

**_Ability Score Increase._** Your Charisma score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**_Charming Smile._** You can cast the _[[Charm Person]]_ spell without expending a spell slot a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Long Rest]].

**_Rationalize._** When spells or effects that cause the [[Charmed]] condition end, the target doesn’t know it was Charmed by you.
