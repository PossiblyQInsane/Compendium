_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 7+, Kindred Class)_

---

Your skin is dense and difficult to damage. You gain the following benefits.

**_Ability Score Increase._** Your Constitution score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**_Damage Resistance._** Select Bludgeoning or Slashing damage. You have [[Resistance]] to the chosen damage.
