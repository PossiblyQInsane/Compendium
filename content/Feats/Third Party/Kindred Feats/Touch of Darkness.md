_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 2+, Kindred Class)_

---

There is darkness within you, and it has given you a gift. You gain the following benefits.

**_Eyes of Night._** You can see normally in [[Dim Light]] and [[Darkness]]—both magical and nonmagical—within 120 feet of yourself.

**_Shadowplay._** You have [[Advantage]] on Dexterity ([[Stealth]]) checks.
