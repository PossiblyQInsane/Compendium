_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 2+, Kindred Class)_

---

You have an animalistic connection to the creatures of the wilderness. You gain the following benefits.

**_Call of the Wild._** As a [[Magic]] action, you can expend 1 Blood Point to summon a number of Large or smaller Beasts (such as a **[[Rat]]** or **[[Wolf]]**) equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of 1). The total Challenge Rating for summoned Beasts can’t exceed your Proficiency Bonus. A summoned Beast arrives at your location in 1d4 + 1 rounds and is [[Charmed]] by you for 1 minute after it arrives. In combat, a Beast shares your Initiative count, but it takes its turn immediately after yours. It obeys your verbal commands (no action required by you). If you don’t issue any, it takes the [[Dodge]] action and uses its movement to avoid danger.

**_Shared Spirit._** You can cast the _[[Speak with Animals]]_ spell, but only as a [[Ritual]].
