_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Kindred Class)_
***
You learn the basic concepts of a vampiric Discipline from another clan or bloodline. You gain one of the following Disciplines of your choice.

**Animalism.** You gain proficiency in the [[Animal Handling]] and [[Nature]] skills. If you already have proficiency in that skill, you gain [[Expertise]] in it.

**Auspex.** You gain proficiency in the [[Investigation]] and [[Perception]] skills. If you already have proficiency in that skill, you gain Expertise in it.

**Celerity.** You gain an extra [[Reaction]], which you can use with any of your existing Reactions.

**Dominate.** You gain proficiency in the [[Deception]] and [[Intimidation]] skills. If you already have proficiency in that skill, you gain Expertise in it.

**Fortitude.** Damage dealt to you except Fire and Radiant is reduced by an amount equal to your Proficiency Bonus.

**Obfuscate.** You gain proficiency in the [[Sleight of Hand]] and [[Stealth]] skills. If you already have proficiency in that skill, you gain Expertise in it.

**Oblivion.** You can see normally in [[Dim Light]] and [[Darkness]]—both magical and nonmagical—within 90 feet of yourself.

**Potence.** When you hit a creature with a Melee weapon or [[Unarmed Strike]] as part of the [[Attack]] action on your turn, you can deal extra damage to the target equal to your Proficiency Bonus.

**Presence.** You gain proficiency in the [[Performance]] and [[Persuasion]] skills. If you already have proficiency in that skill, you gain Expertise in it.

**Protean.** You can deal an extra 1d6 Slashing damage to a target you hit with an [[Unarmed Strike]].

**_Repeatable._** You can take this feat more than once, but you must choose a different Discipline each time.
