_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 2+, Kindred Class)_

---

You manage your Beast better than many. You gain the following benefits.

**_Ability Score Increase._** Your Wisdom score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**_Reining the Beast._** When you would be affected by the Hunger of your Beast feature, you can take a [[Reaction]] to immediately end the effect.

You can use this benefit a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once). You regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Long Rest]].
