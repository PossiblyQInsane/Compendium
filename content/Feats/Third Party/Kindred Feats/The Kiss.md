_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Kindred Class)_

---

Your bite is intoxicating to your prey.

**_Pleasurable Feeding._** When you feed, you can choose to roll d4s instead of d6s for your Feed Dice. When you do so, you regain 1 Blood Point on a roll of a 4 on the d4, and the creature is [[Charmed]] by you. The Charmed condition lasts until the end of your next turn.
