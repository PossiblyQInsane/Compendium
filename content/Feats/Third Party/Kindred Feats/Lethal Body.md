_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Kindred Class)_

---

You possess potent vampiric strength, turning your bare hands into deadly weapons.

**_Crushing Blows._** You can deal 1d8 extra damage when you hit a target with an [[Unarmed Strike]]. The extra damage is the same type dealt by that strike.

**_Iron Grip._** A creature has [[Disadvantage]] on ability checks and saving throws to escape your grapple.
