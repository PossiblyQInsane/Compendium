_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 2+, Kindred Class)_
***
You can dominate others with your mental powers. You gain the following benefits.

**_Cloud Memory._** You can cast the _[[Command]]_ spell without expending a spell slot a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Long Rest]]. In addition, you gain access to a special command.

**_Forget._** The target has only a hazy and unsure recollection of the last 5 minutes.
