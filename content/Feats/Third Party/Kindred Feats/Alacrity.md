_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 2+, Kindred Class)_

---

You move at supernatural speeds. You gain the following benefits.

**_Burst of Speed._** As a [[Bonus Action]], you can expend 1 Blood Point to gain an additional action. That action can be used to take only the [[Attack]] (one attack only) or [[Disengage]] action.

**_Fast._** Your Speed increases by 10 feet, and you have [[Advantage]] on Initiative rolls.
