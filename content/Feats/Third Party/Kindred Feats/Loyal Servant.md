_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 7+, Kindred Class)_

---

You have a loyal companion who has gained power from your blood. You gain the following benefits.

**_Companion._** A loyal Beast or Humanoid is Blood Bound to you. Choose a Beast or Humanoid stat block that has a maximum Challenge Rating of up to half your Proficiency Bonus. When choosing a companion, you may look in the monster section of source books or elsewhere for an eligible Beast or Humanoid if the Game Master permits you to do so.

**_Power in the Blood._** Your Loyal Servant has one Kindred feat of your choice, and it counts as a level 2 Kindred for the purpose of selecting a Kindred feat. In addition, your Loyal Servant can spend 1 [[Hit Point Dice|Hit Point Die]] in place of 1 Blood Point for the purpose of its feat.

**_Replacing a Servant._** If your servant is killed or otherwise leaves, you can choose a new Loyal Servant after 30 days. The GM can lengthen or shorten this time based on in-game circumstances.
