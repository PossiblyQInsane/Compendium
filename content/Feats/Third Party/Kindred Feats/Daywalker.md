_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Kindred Class)_

---

The sun’s light doesn’t harm you as badly as it does other Kindred. You gain the following benefits.

**_Dhampir’s Boon._** When you take damage from sunlight, reduce the damage taken by 2.

**_Endure Radiance._** As a [[Bonus Action]], you can expend 1 Blood Point to remove your [[Vulnerability]] to Radiant damage for 1 minute.

**_Wake with Evening’s Freshness._** When you start a [[Long Rest]], you can make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw. If you succeed, you count as having at least 1 Blood Point for that Long Rest.
