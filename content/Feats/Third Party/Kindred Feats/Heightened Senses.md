_Kindred Feat (Prerequisite: Level 2+, Kindred Class)_

---

Your vampiric senses are stronger and more precise than ever before. You gain the following benefits.

**_Preternatural Perception._** You have [[Advantage]] on Wisdom ([[Insight]] and [[Perception]]) checks.

**_Sense the Unseen._** You have [[Blindsight]] with a range of 30 feet. In addition, you can cast the _[[See Invisibility]]_ spell without expending a spell slot a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Long Rest]].
