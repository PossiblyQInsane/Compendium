_Fighting Style Feat (Prerequisite: Fighting Style Feature)_

---

When you make an extra attack as a result of using a weapon that has the [[Light (Property)|Light]] property, you can add your ability modifier to the damage of that attack if you aren’t already adding it to the damage.
